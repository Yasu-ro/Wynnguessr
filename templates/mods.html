Features

3D Map

Press M and the camera flies up into a block-by-block relief of the province, in its true colours, with everything from Wynntils' map on top.

The relief

The whole of Wynntils' map, rebuilt block by block, in true colours with Minecraft's shading and fog. Bridges, tree tops and the sky islands float; cliffs show their sides. {% call info('the relief') %}Three detail levels, switched liveLow: big cells of 8 blocks.Medium: a point every 2 blocks.Max: every single block, and you can zoom closer.A dark outline marks every edge, so it stays readable at any zoom.{% endcall %}

Everything from Wynntils

Places, travel, services, merchants, dungeons, caves, loot chests, your waypoints and markers. Wynntils draws them itself, so they look and work just like on its 2D map. {% call info('the icons') %}12 kinds of iconsShow or hide each kind from the left bar.Hover an icon to see how far it is; the ground under your cursor gives its X, Z.Buttons at the top find the nearest blacksmith, identifier or potion merchant.A heatmap shows where to gather: mining, woodcutting, farming and fishing, by level.{% endcall %}

Party & friends

See where your party and friends are, how far away, and near which place. Click someone to fly there, right-click to follow them with a marker. {% call info('party and friends') %}Only players who use Wynntils and share their location show up.Optional beams in their colour, visible in game even with the map closed (on Wynncraft only).{% endcall %}

Your way

21 settings, from the gear button or Mod Menu: the opening flight, the controls, the detail, the icons, the fog, the sounds. {% call info('the settings') %}OpeningFlight on or off and its speed, north up, open on you or on your last view, starting zoom and tilt (down to straight from above).ControlsRotation and zoom speed, zoom toward the cursor, invert scroll, smoothing.DisplayDetail, icon size, fog, outlines, coordinates, nearest-service buttons, heatmap opacity, sounds.Party & friendsIn-game beams, for the party only or for friends too.{% endcall %}

Light on your PC

While the map is open, the world isn't drawn at all, so it often runs faster than the game itself. {% call info('performance') %}Measured on the test PC at 1600×900. With Photon shaders, the game went from about 240 fps to 700 to 1,300 fps with the map open.Max uses about 220 MB of RAM and up to about 530 MB of video memory. Low and Medium use far less (96 and 192 MB of video memory).{% endcall %}

Install

Needs Minecraft 1.21.11, Fabric Loader 0.19+, Fabric API and Wynntils 4.2. {% call info('what it needs') %}Tested with Wynntils 4.2.16.Mod Menu is optional: it adds a Configure button.{% endcall %}

  1. Put the .jar in your mods folder.
  2. Put carte.wdc (12 MB, the map) in config/wynndleplus. Create the folder. {% call info('Max detail') %}For Max detail, add carte_max.wdc (37 MB) in the same folder. Without it, Max uses the normal map.{% endcall %}
  3. In game, press M. {% call info('the M key') %}Wynntils' own map is on M too: move one of them in Controls › Wynndle+. The Wynndle+ modpack puts Wynntils' map on the comma.{% endcall %}

Nothing to install on the server: it works on Wynncraft as it is.

Fair play

Made around Wynncraft's mod rules {% call info('the rules') %}Forum thread 111874.{% endcall %}

  • No freecam: the game camera never moves.
  • No cave maps: caves and closed rooms stay solid.
  • Nothing outside the official map.
  • No radar: no mobs, no loot alerts.
  • Players only come from Wynntils' own sharing.

Good to know

  • The terrain is a snapshot: new builds show up when the map is rebuilt.
  • Fruma isn't on the map yet.
  • No bank button: Wynntils' data has no banks.
  • "Open on last view" lasts until you close the game.
  • A Wynntils update can turn the icons off. The map keeps working.

Thanks to Wynntils (icons, waypoints, markers, player sharing; the mod needs it), WynnLODGrabber by DrBiznes and Distant Horizons (the terrain), and Wynncraft (the world itself). {% call info('how it is made') %}The terrain comes from the Distant Horizons LOD pack published by WynnLODGrabber (release LOD-04-19-26).Our server pulls out the surface, the sides of the blocks and the underside of floating things, then takes the colours from the block textures.{% endcall %}
