Game Modes

On Your Own

Survival main mode

You start with hearts: 10, 5 or just 1. Guess inside the green zone and you lose nothing; the further out, the more hearts you lose. The zone shrinks every round, a creeper waits for the end of the clock, and from round 4 potions get thrown at you.

How many places can you survive?

{{ (pin | format('#4363d8')) | safe }}

Classic

The original game: 3 to 25 rounds, up to 5,000 points each, more the closer you are. Pick the provinces, and add a timer or lock the view for a challenge.

Beat your best score.

Play Solo

With Friends

Survival

Everyone plays the same place at once. Good guesses earn emeralds; between rounds, spend them at the Potion Merchant and throw potions at the others.

Be the last one standing.

Yasuro creator

I just wanted to pick up someone else's project, Ched3's WynnGuessr. Then I kept going, and going: new modes, playing with friends, a whole new look, and now a 3D map mod. The goal is simple: something fully free for the Wynncraft community.

{# his own words, answered before anyone asks #}

Yes, I use AI

Mostly as a tool. It exists, and it would be sad not to use it: it makes building things way faster, and it catches what a human eye can miss, especially when it comes to security.

I can read code, I can write code, I've been at it for 5 years.

But I'll be honest: I absolutely suck at CSS. That's where AI saves me the most.
